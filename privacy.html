FacturApp privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

FacturApp is a private, internal invoicing tool operated by Agence Windgrowth inc. (Quebec, Canada). It is not offered to the public. Its QuickBooks Online integration connects only to QuickBooks companies its operator controls or is authorized to use, in order to create invoices in them.

What we access

When a QuickBooks Online company is connected, FacturApp reads its customers, products and services, and sales tax codes, and creates invoices in it. It does not read bank accounts, payroll, or reports.

What we store

  • The QuickBooks company ID and the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to keep the connection open.
  • The identifiers and numbers of the invoices FacturApp creates, so that it never creates the same invoice twice.
  • The selected customer, item and tax code identifiers.

This data is kept in the operator's private database and its backups, and is used only to create those invoices.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share QuickBooks data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.

Disconnecting and deletion

A connected company can disconnect FacturApp at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings, Apps) or from within FacturApp. Disconnecting revokes access. To have the stored tokens and identifiers deleted, write to the address below.
